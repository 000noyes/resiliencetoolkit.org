    <td colSpan={2}>
      <strong>Water supply and storage</strong>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <p>
        If your municipality's central water system is down, plan for how to source large volumes
        of water via wells or water trucks, and distribute via water stations in the community.
      </p>
      <div class="guide-table__checklist">
        <Todo id="identify-water-sources" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          Identify local clean and potable water sources
        </Todo>
        <Todo id="store-water" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          Store 2-5 days of water
        </Todo>
        <Todo id="rotate-water" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          Rotate water stock every 6-12 months and store out of the heat and extreme cold.
        </Todo>
        <Todo id="backup-fill-method" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          Back-up method to fill up water jugs.
        </Todo>
        <Todo id="test-water" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          Test your water; test kits are available thru VT Department of Health.
        </Todo>
      </div>
    </td>
    <td>
      <strong>Water storage vessels</strong>
      <div class="guide-table__checklist">

      <strong>Community meals</strong>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <p>
        Provide meals for communities during disaster and recovery. Feeding people is key to
        sustaining response and relief efforts.
      </p>
      <div class="guide-table__checklist">
        <Todo id="existing-meal-providers" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          If there are existing providers of community meals, ask them to cook large meals for
          distribution
        </Todo>
        <Todo id="meal-outreach" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          Conduct outreach to ask for meals from nearby restaurants, organizations and community
          meals in neighboring towns.
        </Todo>
        <Todo id="kitchen-directory" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VO1V7xknP-Ygvz7m_JL9m1kL7paFtuFP/view?usp=drive_link">Create a
          directory</ExternalLink> of, or <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1HZSXmTaX1jc3IlZps-4PayHkrrwDEIRu">set up
          kitchens</ExternalLink> in your community available for creating both community meals and possibly meal trains for
          individual household use. Local churches or restaurants might be able to offer access to
          a commercial kitchen, reusable plates and silverware, etc.
        </Todo>
        <Todo id="cooks-directory" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1at2VF06qMMKi3olVD4uNecniZZFnjKz0/view?usp=drive_link">Create a directory of
          cooks</ExternalLink> who are prepared to meal share (cook extra) for households who have lost access to
          kitchens, food, water, etc
        </Todo>
        <Todo id="food-distribution-system" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          Establish a system to distribute food at volunteer coordination and supply pickup sites
        </Todo>
        <Todo id="meal-schedule" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          Set a daily and weekly schedule for meals, and get volunteers to sign-up for making or
          distributing meals
        </Todo>
        <Todo id="source-food-schedule" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          Source food to fill in distribution dates and times
        </Todo>
        <Todo id="safe-food-handling" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          Ensure <ExternalLink href="https://www.cdc.gov/foodsafety/communication/food-safety-tips-during-emergencies.html">safe food
          handling</ExternalLink> at distribution centers - refrigeration/coolers for things to stay cold vs. crock pots
          and portable stoves to keep things warm.
        </Todo>
        <Todo id="food-access-directory" moduleKey="food-and-water" client:idle>
          <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1HZSXmTaX1jc3IlZps-4PayHkrrwDEIRu">Create a directory of food access
          points</ExternalLink>, using a spreadsheet, <ExternalLink href="http://tinyurl.com/freefoodbtv">google map</ExternalLink>, or <ExternalLink href="https://cdn.flowcode.com/prodassets/NEKCA_FoodAccess_Summer.24.2.pdf">other
          format</ExternalLink>. Include resources like food shelves, free food distributions and meal sites, general and grocery
          stores, farmers' markets.
        </Todo>
      </div>
