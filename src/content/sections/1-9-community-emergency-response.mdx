<section>
  <h2 class="text-title font-semibold mb-4">Before disaster</h2>

  <div class="space-y-4">
    <Todo id="identify-local-emp-docs" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Identify if there are already local documents outlining the emergency response plan for your town — including plans from the Emergency Management Director (<ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ds67bbFqr4C5ZsaBgJFVusylEhogWepo/view?usp=drive_link">LEMPs</ExternalLink>), Fire Department, First Responders/EMS, Town Clerk, Select Board Members, Town Road Crew, and Mutual Aid/Neighbor-to-Neighbor Network leaders.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="create-emergency-manager-directory" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1T53T9L9naTi0IibuIFdXohQXkCHitwZ3/view?usp=drive_link">Create a directory of emergency managers</ExternalLink>, municipal leaders, and heads of neighbor-to-neighbor groups to coordinate and communicate at the town-wide level. The 911 EMS agency covering your town should have a list of local first-responders.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="share-directory" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Share the directory with everyone listed in it, or organize an event where everyone can meet and strategize around what to do when disaster strikes.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="communication-system" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Create a clear communication system specifically for emergency response.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="neighborhood-pods" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Organize neighborhood pods with block captain/<ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1k7SXv4md2Kjh0t-nGx5xIKVPpg0bSPmn/view?usp=drive_link">phone tree systems</ExternalLink> if there is phone service.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="neighbor-directory" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1volnFb9p7K24iROikMt_zOg9_RT9WRVR/view?usp=drive_link">Create a directory of neighbors</ExternalLink> with street addresses, landline contacts, etc.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="first-responder-directory" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_s3dzOMkIUT5BGBFjfFlEDeHfHcc5m-N/view?usp=drive_link">Create a directory of local first responders</ExternalLink>, paid and volunteer.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="ham-radio" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Identify people trained in HAM radio.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="evacuation-plan" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Identify an evacuation plan and options for sheltering nearby if evacuation is not possible.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="volunteer-sweep-plan" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Create a plan for volunteers to physically sweep known problem areas — EMS does not monitor these.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="pre-outreach-team" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Identify a core team to do pre-outreach by town or volunteer/supply hub to <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1kFn8Lmia51X-Y5ZCzu2VlsWJd7JEq1dy/view?usp=drive_link">share information about preparedness</ExternalLink> and distribute info on available resources.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="mutual-aid-group" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      If none exists, establish a neighbor-to-neighbor or mutual aid group in your town. Consider partnering with an existing group that already holds relationships. EMS and some municipalities often have lists of vulnerable people — make sure those folk are known to the neighbor-to-neighbor group.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="language-access" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Identify language access needs and utilize tools like the <ExternalLink href="https://www.vtlanguagejustice.org/ify?">VT Language Justice Project app</ExternalLink> to support communication during emergencies.
    </Todo>
  </div>
</section>

<section>
  <h2 class="text-title font-semibold mb-4">During disaster</h2>

  <div class="space-y-4">
    <Todo id="daily-stakeholder-meetings" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Hold daily meetings with key stakeholders (Mutual Aid Orgs, EMS, EMD, selectboard, etc.) and create <ExternalLink href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1p7EPPThPDA--5NxEKXIi6MZDzHthwg8VGi5XX1tq9Ek/edit?usp=drive_link">situation reports</ExternalLink> about actions taken during emergency response.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="public-assemblies" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Hold regular public assemblies at an accessible time and place. Share easy-to-absorb information and distribute things people need (food, water, fuel). Advertise with posters, word of mouth, phone trees, Front Porch Forum, social media, and local listservs.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="resident-request-tracker" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Create hand-outs or digital surveys to capture requests from impacted residents, integrating into a tracker for case management.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="water-monitoring" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Use weather maps at <ExternalLink href="https://waterdata.usgs.gov/vt/nwis/rt">waterdata.usgs.gov/vt/nwis/rt</ExternalLink> and the <ExternalLink href="https://www.riveraware.app/">River Aware app</ExternalLink>. Identify people to watch the river for ice and debris dams.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="emergency-alerts" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Receive and submit alerts at <ExternalLink href="https://vem.vermont.gov/vtalert">vem.vermont.gov/vtalert</ExternalLink> and TextMyGov.
    </Todo>
  </div>
</section>

<section>
  <h2 class="text-title font-semibold mb-4">Planning for other kinds of climate disaster</h2>

  <p class="mb-4">While flooding is Vermont's most common climate disaster, consider other potential events in emergency plans.</p>

  <div class="space-y-4">
    <Todo id="wildfire-smoke-supplies" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1W8BnUVz9kKa5PFywiWXJgacrnKS93ssK/view">Wildfire smoke</ExternalLink>: Store N95s/KN95s/respirators with emergency supplies. Monitor air quality via <ExternalLink href="https://www.airnow.gov/">AirNow.gov</ExternalLink>.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="wildfire-smoke-shelters" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Identify public spaces with air filtration (libraries, schools, health centers) for people without home air filtration during smoke events.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="heat-advisories" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/10X2ipgJTjW1SdCaNbJZfEOI4ukD8UVYz/view">Wet bulb temperatures and high heat</ExternalLink>: Communicate across community networks when heat advisories are in effect. Remind people about hydration and create access to drinking water in public places on hot days.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="cooling-shelters" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      Identify cooling shelters and encourage people to stay in shade or cool locations during the hottest part of the day (11am–3pm). Learn and share signs of heat exhaustion.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="drought-fire-danger" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1glq_jPse9R4geIkeaCUr7rnAgNG5JOyW/view">Drought</ExternalLink>: Monitor fire danger at <ExternalLink href="https://fpr.vermont.gov/forest/wildland-fire/monitoring-fire-danger">fpr.vermont.gov/forest/wildland-fire/monitoring-fire-danger</ExternalLink>. Conserve water by reducing lawn watering, turning off taps, and reducing toilet flushing.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="ice-wind-storm-prep" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/10xdbWN2CSxa_C-__rtC2H4iSBzgDVO-s/view">Ice and wind storms</ExternalLink>: Store emergency food and water (1.2) and back-up power/heating (1.4). Identify local warming shelters (1.5) ahead of storms. Plan back-up communications (radios, walkies, default meetup plans) in case electricity and phones go down.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="pandemic-preparedness" moduleKey="community-emergency-response" client:idle>
      <ExternalLink href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_e3AA4pD7dJCAcfMHLVJ3dqJgK5_5Wrc/view?usp=drive_link">Public health emergencies</ExternalLink>: Keep people healthy as a baseline. Create community emergency response plans, support populations with specific needs (1.8), and set up mutual aid networks (1.12). Stay up to date with information from local, state, and federal sources.
    </Todo>
  </div>
</section>
