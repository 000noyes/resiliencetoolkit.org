<section>
  <h2 class="text-title font-semibold mb-4">Tool lending library</h2>

  <div class="space-y-4">
    <Todo id="tool-directory" moduleKey="shared-tools" client:idle>
      Identify useful, higher cost, sharable tools and equipment that could be gathered in one space or a people with tools who are willing to lend them. Create a directory of this information.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="tool-library-expansion" moduleKey="shared-tools" client:idle>
      If your area established a tool lending system during disaster response, consider building up a regularly available library for on-going use. See sections 1.3, 1.6, 1.11 for lists of tools useful for disaster response and recovery.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="tool-library-research" moduleKey="shared-tools" client:idle>
      Read up on best practices and existing ways of doing this. New Suns Community Center (Thetford) and Cooperation Vermont (Marshfield) have tool libraries. See also: <ExternalLink href="https://www.shareable.net/library-of-things-toolkit/#anchor_Section%2011:%20Workshops%20and%20classes">Library of Things toolkit from Shareable</ExternalLink>, <ExternalLink href="https://myturn.com/">myTurn</ExternalLink> (online tool for managing tool sharing), and <ExternalLink href="https://www.toollibraryalliance.org/">Tool Library Alliance</ExternalLink> for lots of resources.
    </Todo>
  </div>
</section>

<section>
  <h2 class="text-title font-semibold mb-4">Internet and phone access</h2>

  <div class="space-y-4">
    <Todo id="internet-access" moduleKey="shared-tools" client:idle>
      Identify areas for public or low-cost internet access when home systems are down. This is commonly <ExternalLink href="https://libraries.vermont.gov/sites/libraries/files/documents/PublicLibraryDirectory.pdf">libraries</ExternalLink>, town offices and schools.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="internet-local-businesses" moduleKey="shared-tools" client:idle>
      Consider reaching out to local businesses that could be made more accessible during disaster (maybe in the town over or a less flood-prone area).
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="internet-solar-access" moduleKey="shared-tools" client:idle>
      Consider investing in a few portable, solar back-up power units as well as power banks that can be stored and ready in public spaces with wifi to create hotspots if phone service is cut out but internet can be accessed. Use tools like the Footprint Project for resources.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="internet-mesh-networks" moduleKey="shared-tools" client:idle>
      <ExternalLink href="https://docs.engnroom.org/digital-resilience-hub/meaningful-connectivity/alternative-tech-tools-for-knowledge-sharing/mesh-and-community-networks">Mesh Networks</ExternalLink> can be established with limited required infrastructure and offer localized peer to peer secure communication and data sharing. Some examples: <ExternalLink href="https://fognet.me/">Fognet</ExternalLink> network, <ExternalLink href="https://meshtastic.org/docs/introduction/">Meshtastic</ExternalLink> - Low-power data sharing & communications network, <ExternalLink href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BpSkjRKGxyc">DIY Cellular Signal Booster</ExternalLink> - No Electricity Required. <ExternalLink href="https://docs.engnroom.org/digital-resilience-hub">More Information on digital resilience here.</ExternalLink>
    </Todo>
  </div>
</section>
