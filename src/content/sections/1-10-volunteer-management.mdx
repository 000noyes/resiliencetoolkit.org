<section>
  <h2 class="text-title font-semibold mb-4">Recruiting volunteers</h2>

  <div class="space-y-4">
    <Todo id="post-volunteer-opportunities" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Post about volunteer opportunities on Front Porch Forum, social media, and in your local paper.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="restaurant-meals" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      See if a local restaurant can provide meals to volunteers.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="volunteer-org-list" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Make lists of organizations and individuals willing to volunteer and/or coordinate volunteers during disaster: mutual aid networks, local companies offering employee volunteer hours, faith communities, schools, American Legion, Elks Club, Rotary Club, and <ExternalLink href="https://www.healthvermont.gov/about-us/contact-us">AHS/VDH regional field directors</ExternalLink>.
    </Todo>
  </div>
</section>

<section>
  <h2 class="text-title font-semibold mb-4">Setting up volunteer shifts</h2>

  <div class="space-y-4">
    <Todo id="signup-tool" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Use <ExternalLink href="https://www.signupgenius.com/nonprofit-volunteer-management">SignUp Genius</ExternalLink> (free) or a <ExternalLink href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1sJ-inMiVKj5SWsCukg_IimgWcA4oQLVWkbg6lAxcI3E/edit?gid=1323271388#gid=1323271388&range=A9">shareable spreadsheet</ExternalLink> to create volunteer shifts. Download your data regularly to maintain a contact list.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="specific-time-slots" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Create specific time slots and tasks for people to help with.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="physical-requirements" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Be clear on what physical abilities people need to be safe and helpful.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="diverse-tasks" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Have a diversity of tasks to make space for everyone to engage. Assess if projects are within your team's scope — the home might be structurally unsound, you may be unable to get permission from a landlord, etc.
    </Todo>
  </div>
</section>

<section>
  <h2 class="text-title font-semibold mb-4">Managing volunteers</h2>

  <div class="space-y-4">
    <Todo id="children-policy" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Handle children at volunteer sites on a case-by-case basis. Do not send children to locations with safety concerns such as mold.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="crisis-cleanup" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Know what work needs to get done. Use <ExternalLink href="http://crisiscleanup.org">Crisis Cleanup</ExternalLink> and track projects in a spreadsheet.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="homeowner-interviews" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Conduct interviews with homeowners to determine scope of work (this can itself be a volunteer task). Interview questions are pre-loaded in Crisis Cleanup.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="volunteer-worksheets" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Create a work sheet for volunteers with info about where they're going, who they're meeting, and what they're doing.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="volunteer-waivers" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      For legal and insurance reasons, have flood volunteers <ExternalLink href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TNQQW3tC_uiBaEp6ndfhjITBqmlt39CU/edit?usp=drive_link&ouid=106837070816865731479&rtpof=true&sd=true">sign a waiver</ExternalLink> before starting work. Link to the agreement wherever volunteers sign up for shifts.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="volunteer-supplies-info" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Tell volunteers what to bring (sunscreen, water, snacks, durable footwear, etc.).
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="volunteer-training" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Train volunteers in the tasks assigned each shift. Include the importance of taking breaks and hydrating.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="work-crews" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Organize volunteers into work crews of 3–6 people, large enough so people can take breaks and not work more than 5 hours at a stretch.
    </Todo>

    <Todo id="feed-volunteers" moduleKey="volunteer-management" client:idle>
      Feed volunteers and find ways to thank and appreciate them.
    </Todo>
  </div>
</section>
